# Adaptive learning and final assessment

- Apply this policy exclusively to Study Desk, across note-backed chats, chats without notes, graded-paper review chats, starter prompts, retries, and suggestion-triggered questions. Keep the editor's standalone AI assistant independent. Suggestions must respect the same classification and lesson stage, and automatic test recommendations must wait for assessment. Explicit New Test, flashcard generation, transcription, and grading remain the requested operations; they must not trigger unsolicited lessons or additional tests.
- Route manageable Study Desk requests through an accessible, configured x1 model, preferring the selected answer model's provider. Classify scope by meaning: a large concept such as calculus needs a lesson; a specific calculation or definition needs a direct answer.
- For large concepts, the lightweight model supplies 2–12 ordered learning pieces with goals, retrieval terms, and fresh foundational checks. The user's selected model teaches one piece per turn. Preserve source scope and existing oversized-source clarification.
- After the last piece is understood or the student asks to continue, move into a final assessment. The selected model gives 3–5 fresh numbered questions covering the lesson, withholding solutions until attempted. Review partial submissions without exposing unanswered solutions; complete after all questions have been attempted or the student explicitly ends the test. Completion does not certify mastery.
- For small problems and side questions, the selected model answers directly without appended tests, quizzes, exercises, or understanding checks. Preserve any existing lesson position and assessment stage. Explicitly requested practice is still allowed.
- Support lessons with no attached notes. Never invent source citations. Persist learning, assessment, and completed stages with the session; interpret older plans as learning. Reject early assessments, skipped steps, invalid stages, and automatic test restarts.
- Commit progress only after a successful answer. Preserve retries, request isolation, usage accounting, and fallback answers when a lightweight model is unavailable or fails. Keep planner output out of the visible transcript.
- Verify routing and provider preference, focused-question behavior, complete lesson/test flows with and without notes, restored assessment state, partial grading, completion, failed assessment retries, and invalid transitions.
