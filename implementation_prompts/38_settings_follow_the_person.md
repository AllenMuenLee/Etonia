# Chats, configured AI and handwriting fonts follow the person

- The Study Desk chats (and courses), the configured AI (thinking effort, models turned off, "show model used", preferred model) and the handwriting fonts (the font chosen or the writer's own, the AI text size, the calibration choices) are backed up to, and brought in from, whichever destination is chosen: Google Drive or iCloud. Nothing about them is sent unless a destination is linked.
- They travel as one `settings-<hash>.json` in the backup's `notes` folder, named by what it holds so a half-sent copy never stands in for it. The manifest names it as `settings` (`fileId`, `hash`); a backup without it, or a manifest from before it, is read as before, and a version that does not know it ignores it. It is taken in before a backup sends anything (as edits made elsewhere are), by a pull from another device, and by a restore. A settings file that cannot be had never stops the notes syncing; the next backup sends it again.
- `SyncedSettings` holds the sections: `StudyChatsSection`, `AiSettingsSection`, `HandwritingFontsSection`. A section reads its data the same way on every device (sorted, nothing that belongs to one device, such as the open chat), writes what it is given into the live controller or store, and decides a merge. When a section last changed here is worked out from its content against what it was when last synced, kept in `settings_sync.json`, shared by both destinations. A section still as a new device has it never wins, so restoring on a new device or opening it for the first time never overwrites a set-up one; otherwise the newest choice wins. What came in is recorded as it was written, so it is not sent back as new.
- Chats are merged, not replaced: every chat on any device is on all of them, the later `updatedAt` of one chat wins, and a chat deleted on one is deleted on all through `deletedSessions` (id and time, kept 180 days; a chat written after its deletion is back). The chat being written and its draft are left alone while an answer is on its way; an empty new chat is never sent or removed.
- A change to any of the three asks for a backup soon (`onSaved` from the desk, the subscription controller's routing and preferred model, the handwriting store's writes) and marks the library dirty for the scheduler. Credits spent are not a change. A new device pulls before it offers handwriting calibration (waiting at most a few seconds), so a set-up font is not asked for again.
