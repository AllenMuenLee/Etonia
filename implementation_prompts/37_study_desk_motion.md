# Study Desk motion

- Every Study Desk element that comes, goes, or changes moves, using the shared motion vocabulary (`AppMotion` springs for movement, durations and curves for opacity and colour, `Pressable` for press feedback, `MotionMemory` for first look versus revisit). Reduced motion keeps only a short fade.
- Between the chat and the practice studio the two stay built and keep their state: only the one in view is painted, ticking and read out, and the move is a fade with a slight slide. A class overview, a chat, a deck, a paper and the material library play in where they are when opened, slowly the first time and as a glance after. Opening the citation pane or the history opens the room it takes and lets go of what it held once shut; both are laid out once at their resting width and only revealed.
- New questions rise in once, and rows are staggered once (`StudyMotionLedger`, bounded, cleared when the system is low on memory). Anything on screen when a chat opens, or read as it streamed in, does not arrive again. Cards, sidebar rows, chips and buttons sink under the finger. Optional blocks (failures, working, search, recommendations, status) ease their room open and shut.
- No jank: a keystroke rebuilds only the composer; streamed chunks rebuild the screen at most about twelve times a second, with the streaming tail in its own repaint boundary; the history sidebar follows the controller at most about six times a second while an answer streams. Moving between layouts keeps the chat's state (global key) instead of building it again. A studio with nothing open is let go on memory pressure.
- Verify with the motion toolkit tests (arrival, replay, reduced motion, presence, scene state, throttling) and the existing Study Desk suites.
- Caching, so reused elements are not made twice: an answer's Markdown and maths are parsed once per answer and look and handed back as the same widgets; transcript rows are rebuilt only when their message, position, or chip labels change; PDF pages rasterized for the pane, note images, and diagram crops live in byte-budgeted LRU caches (`StudyViewCache`, keyed by repository, note, revision and page, one shared run per key, cleared on memory pressure, nothing over a third of a budget kept). A cache hit shows at once; a miss is covered by a fade (paper over ink, crop over its placeholder, page over its loader).
- Layout is never animated: the history and the citation pane are laid out once at their resting size and only slid or revealed over the chat, which takes its resting width once. The practice studio, kept built behind the chat, follows the controller only while in view; the history list builds only visible rows; the streaming refresh gap grows with the answer's length.
