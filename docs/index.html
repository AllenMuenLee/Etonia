Inside Etonia

Built around the sheet of paper, not a chat box.

Every screen in Etonia stays quiet—two-tone ink and paper—until you lay down highlighter strokes or summon the AI assistant. Here is how Etonia works from first stroke to final export.

AI Assistant & Canvas Notation

Lasso a problem. Get handwritten steps and graphs right on your page.

Instead of copying formulas into a separate chatbot window, circle any region of your note with the Lasso tool. Etonia's multimodal AI sees your exact strokes, checks your math or physics derivation, and writes annotations directly onto your canvas.

  • ✓ One-tap quick prompts: Explain this, Complete this, Grade this, and Clean up handwriting.
  • ✓ Live function plotting: Automatically sketches coordinate axes, curves, roots, and vertices alongside your work.
  • ✓ Editable vector strokes: Every AI annotation is real vector ink you can move, recolor, or erase with a single undo.
Personal Handwriting Engine

AI that writes in your own handwriting.

Typed text boxes break the flow of a handwritten notebook. Etonia synthesizes fluid vector pen strokes with natural baseline rhythm, fraction bars, radicals, and superscripts—matched to your personal hand.

  • ✓ Five built-in hands: Switch effortlessly between Normal, Cursive, Print, Rounded, and Quick.
  • ✓ 60-second calibration: Write 15 sample characters once and Etonia adapts its stroke slant, roundness, and spacing to match your style.
  • ✓ Clean up messy notes: Select hurried lecture scribbles and reflow them into neat, legible handwriting without losing your voice.
120 Hz Native Ink Studio

Zero-lag Apple Pencil & S Pen ink, instant shapes, and sticky notes.

Etonia's custom ink engine captures full-rate stylus coalesced and predicted touches on iOS and Android, stabilized by an adaptive One Euro Filter and rendered on the GPU with Impeller.

  • ✓ Expressive toolset: Ballpoint, Fountain, and Highlighter brushes plus stroke/pixel Eraser, Lasso, Image, Text, and Sticky Notes.
  • ✓ Hold-to-shape recognition: Pause at the end of a stroke to snap freehand lines, circles, rectangles, triangles, and arrows into crisp geometry.
  • ✓ Movable sticky notes: Drop lined yellow sticky notes onto any page for formulas, reminders, or review checklists—and write right inside them.
Dog-Eared Library & Sync

Every notebook organized, searchable, and backed up.

Your library displays live first-page thumbnails with Etonia's signature folded top-right dog-ear. Group classes into nested folders, star daily notebooks, and switch between Light Paper and Dark Ink at any time.

  • ✓ Bring your existing notes: Import PDFs, images, Etonia archives (.etonote), and Goodnotes (.goodnotes) notebooks directly.
  • ✓ Automatic cloud backup: Background sync to your personal Google Drive or iCloud Drive keeps every stroke safe across devices.
  • ✓ Instant local storage: All notes live offline-first on your device in a fast SQLite database—no internet required in lecture halls.
Multi-Page & Infinite Canvas

Mix A4, Letter, Square, and Infinite Whiteboard pages in one note.

Open the Pages popover to skim miniature previews of every sheet, jump between problems, or add new pages with independent paper templates and background colors.

  • ✓ Four paper patterns: Blank, Lined, Grid, and Dotted—each rendered as crisp procedural vector geometry at any zoom level.
  • ✓ Per-page formats: Combine structured A4/Letter lecture pages with an Infinite Whiteboard sheet for mind-mapping.
  • ✓ Lossless vector export: Share multi-page PDFs, crisp SVG vectors, high-DPI PNGs, or editable .etonote bundles.

Custom Paper & Page Formats

Start any note in A4, US Letter, Square, or Infinite Whiteboard with Blank, Lined, Grid, or Dotted templates.

PDF, SVG & Goodnotes Workflow

Configure paper tints, toggle Hold-to-Shape, calibrate your handwriting, or export vector PDF & SVG in two taps.

Design Language & Privacy

Shaped by the folded-E mark.

Etonia's visual identity comes directly from its icon: a bold white E with rounded capsule slots and a folded top-right corner on a deep ink squircle.

Quiet Chrome. Expressive Ink.

The interface has no competing brand hue. Every button, toolbar, and card is monochrome ink and paper so your six highlighters and handwritten diagrams carry 100% of the color.

1

Two-Tone Ink & Paper

High-contrast carbon ink (#0A0A0B) on warm daylight paper (#F2F2F4 / #FFFFFF) in Light mode, and crisp white ink on true OLED black in Dark mode.

2

Fat, Pill-Ended Slots

Inspired by the two horizontal slots cut into the E mark, every primary action, tool selector, and filter chip uses a tactile stadium capsule with spring physics.

3

The Top-Trailing Dog-Ear

The folded corner in the upper-right of the mark turns the letter E into a physical sheet of paper—echoed across note cards in your library and dialogs.

4

On-Device First & Private

Notes are stored locally on your device. AI requests send only the cropped snapshot and stroke context you explicitly select, proxied securely without exposing API keys.
